Simple Process

How CampusMarket Works

Three simple steps to buy or sell anything on campus.

🎓

Sign Up as a Student

Create your account using your university email. Verification keeps the marketplace safe and trusted.

📸

Post Your Listing

Add a photo, write a short description, set your price. Your listing goes live in under 2 minutes — for free.

💬

Chat & Agree

Interested buyers message you directly. Agree on a price and arrange a safe on-campus handover.

🤝

Meet & Exchange

Meet at a designated campus spot. Hand over the item, collect payment, and leave a review. Done!
